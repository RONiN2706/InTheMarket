How to scan it

8–15 seconds is plenty. Good lighting matters more than a fancy camera.

  • Turn the item slowly so the front, back, and both sides are all visible
  • Hold it under a light, not in front of a window or lamp
  • Get close on any scratches, cracks, or dents so they're clearly visible
  • If it powers on, show the screen lit up
